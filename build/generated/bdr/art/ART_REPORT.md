# Dice Rush Extended – generated art report

All files below are generated by the scripts in `tools\art\`
(re-run everything with `tools/venv/Scripts/python.exe tools\art\build_all.py`).
Everything lives under `build\generated\bdr\art\` and is packed as `res://bdr/art/...`.
All PNGs are RGBA with hard alpha (0/255) and clean (zeroed) transparent pixels; `verify.py` checks
sizes, alpha, stray pixels, landmarks and cursor hotspots (result at the end of this file).

## 1. Dice – `dice/<id>.png` (100 × 256x256), `dice/landmarks.json`, `dice/contact_sheet.png`
* Drawn on a native 64x64 grid, upscaled x4 with NEAREST. Same construction for all 100 dice, so the
  landmarks are identical for every die:
  `[[130,8],[244,78],[130,148],[16,78],[16,184],[130,252],[244,184]]`
  (0 top, 1 right, 2 front/centre, 3 left corner of the top rhombus, 4 bottom-left, 5 bottom, 6 bottom-right;
  points lie on the face boundary just inside the 4px outline, accuracy ±1px).
* Geometry copies the original game dice: edge slope 3:5, top rhombus = 57% of the cube height
  (the originals measure 50–62%; the brief's "~45%" would have needed a flatter 2:1 view – the originals won).
* Per die: face colours from the catalog, subtle pattern texture in face-UV space (wood, stone, gem, metal,
  stripes, dots, grid, noise, scales, glitch, stars, bricks, swirl), 2-step light rim on the top face, rims on
  the side faces, dark seams, 3x3 rivets near the 12 face corners (gold rivets for tiers 6–10), twinkle
  sparkles on tiers 8–10 (3 on tier 10), special features (bunny/dog/fox/owl ears, horns, car spoiler).
* Glyphs: pips are laid out in face-UV space (projected); `num:`/`text:` use hand-made extra-bold pixel fonts
  (3px strokes, like the originals' numerals); 139 `icon:` glyphs are hand-made 16px pixel icons
  (`dice_icon_art.py` procedural ones, `dice_icon_art2.py` ASCII ones). Side-face glyphs are sheared with the
  face slope (so they un-warp upright on a 3D cube); the top-face glyph is drawn upright in screen space exactly
  like the original game dice – when the top rhombus is un-warped to a square it appears rotated 45°.
* Light glyphs get a deep face-shade outline (like the original d4), dark glyphs a light halo/emboss (like π).

## 2. Tarot – `tarot/m_<suit>_<n>.png` (40 × 51x79), `tarot/contact_sheet.png` (x3, with 5 originals)
* Frame copied pixel-exact from the original cards (label boxes cleared), exact 4-colour demichrome palette
  (#211e20 #555568 #a0a08b #e9efec) + one accent per suit (wands #78a05a, cups #5f86b8, swords #c4d6ea,
  pentacles #d8b25a). 3x5 pixel font labels: ACE, II…X on top; WANDS / CUPS / SWORDS / PENTACLES at the bottom.
* Pips in a classic playing-card layout over a quiet slate scenery per suit (hills, waves, wind/clouds, garden
  wall); aces are one large ornate symbol inside a halo with rays.
* Cards are fully opaque like the originals (no transparent corners).

## 3. Cursors – `cursors/cursor_1..10.png` (128x128), `cursors/cursors.json`, `cursors/contact_sheet.png`
* 1 golden gauntlet, 2 crystal hand, 3 robot claw, 4 pirate hook, 5 wizard wand + star, 6 cat paw (extended claw),
  7 sword, 8 neon arrow (cyan/pink), 9 skeleton hand, 10 rainbow glove.
* Native 64x64 x2. Like the original cursor_hand.png the content fills the canvas (the game scales the original to
  ~68px; scale these the same way, i.e. ×0.53, and scale the hotspot with it). Black outline + coloured outer rim.
* `cursors.json`: `{"cursor_k": {"hotspot": [x, y], "name": ...}}` – exact tip pixel in 128px space.

## 4. Boards – `boards/board_1..10.png` (1448x1086), `boards/board_k_thumb.png` (290x218), `boards/contact_sheet.png`
* Built from the original `сукно.png`: the frame (wood) and the brass fittings are recoloured with gradient
  maps, so frame geometry, corner plates, diamonds and the felt area (x 78–1370, y 75–1008) are pixel-identical.
* The felt is re-rendered on a 4px pixel grid from the original felt's own noise + inner shadow, plus a theme
  pattern, ordered dithering into 6-colour ramps (no smooth gradients).
* 1 Emerald Casino (green felt / dark wood), 2 Royal Velvet (blue / gold), 3 Purple Galaxy (stars, nebula /
  silver), 4 Deep Ocean (teal waves / driftwood, verdigris fittings), 5 Frozen Ice (cracked ice / frosted),
  6 Lava Rock (dark rock + glowing cracks / obsidian), 7 Desert Sands (dune ripples / sandstone, bronze),
  8 Neon Grid (black + cyan/pink grid / chrome, neon-pink fittings), 9 Gold Luxury (cream + gold lattice /
  black lacquer with gold trim), 10 Iron Checkers (charcoal/grey checks / iron).
* Boards are opaque RGBA (the original is opaque RGB). Thumbnails are box-filtered downscales.

## 5. Icons
* `staff/<id>.png` 160x160 (native 40x40 x4): mechanic, banker, janitor, mage, chef, scientist, merchant, dj,
  miner, monk – chibi workers with their props.
* `events/<id>.png` 160x160: meteor_shower, coin_rain, tornado, happy_hour, rainbow, black_cat, treasure_chest,
  thief, lucky_clover; sprites: coin (64), meteor (64), tornado_sprite (128x160), cat_walk (96x64),
  thief_run (96x96), chest_closed / chest_open (128), clover (96).
* `ui/` 160x160: shop_icon (market stall with a die), unlock_icon, design_icon, coop_icon, host_icon, join_icon,
  invite_icon; `ui/p1_cursor.png` / `ui/p2_cursor.png` 48x48 (blue / orange pointer with a small 1/2 badge;
  the arrow tip is at about (4,4) in the 48px image – use that as the pivot for the remote mouse position).
* Extra request from the coordinator, 128x128 (native 32x32 x4): `ui/lucky_cash.png`, `ui/lucky_free.png`,
  `ui/lucky_boost.png`, `ui/lucky_unlock.png`, `ui/sell_icon.png`, `ui/event_icon.png`.
  NOTE: `ui/sell_icon.png` was in both briefs – it follows the later coordinator spec (128x128, a hand
  dropping a die onto a gold coin pile) instead of 160x160.

## Known limitations
* Dice face glyphs are small pixel drawings (≈16px on a 64px die) – readable at 64px but the busiest
  icons (dragon, nebula, galaxy, kraken, nyan) are simplified silhouettes. Brand/meme dice are original
  stylised drawings (no logos traced).
* The top-face glyph is screen-upright (like the original dice), so a 3D un-warp of the top rhombus shows it
  rotated 45°; ears/horns/spoiler stick out of the cube silhouette and are not part of any face.
* The gem dice family (ruby, sapphire, emerald, amethyst, platinum, obsidian, mithril) shares the same
  gem/10/gem layout from the catalog and differs by colour/pattern only.
* Cursor content fills the 128px canvas (scale it down like the original hand); hotspots are given for 128px.
* Board frames are recoloured, not re-drawn, so every material keeps the original wood-grain relief.
* Previews / review sheets used during development are in `tools\art\_preview\` (not shipped).
